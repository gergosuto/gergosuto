### Work

Professionally, I am interested in issues of (un)sustainable material production and consumption.

I am trained mainly in methods of [industrial ecology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Industrial_ecology), and to smaller extent in electrical engineering, economics, psychology, and design.

Currently I am a consultant at [Alba Concepts](https://albaconcepts.nl), a Dutch consultancy advancing circularity in the construction sector. Here I contribute to sustainability and circularity assessments of buildings and buildings components.

Previously I was an academic researcher at [Utrecht University](https://www.uu.nl) (2024-2026) where I studied the environmental and economic trade-offs of circularity measures for materials like steel, cement, and plastics. Prior to that, I worked at the [Circle Economy](https://www.circle-economy.com) foundation (2023) on assessing the state of the circular economy in countries and cities around the world.

### Photography

*Unprofessionally*, I am interested in photography. I find something incredible about freezing momentary views of my surroundings, and later looking back at these frozen views of mine and others.

This website is the only online display of my photos. It mainly exists as a shorthand when I want to show someone my favourite photos at a party.
